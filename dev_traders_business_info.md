## Market Context

### About Tankshal Market Area

**Historical Background:**
- The area gets its name from a 19th-century haveli that was used as a coin-minting facility during Mughal rule
- "Tankshal" literally means "mint" in Gujarati
- One of the oldest commercial areas in Ahmedabad's walled city

**Market Specialty:**
- Largest wholesale market for imitation jewellery in Ahmedabad
- Major hub for raw materials for ethnic wear
- Extensive party supplies and decorations market
- Over 764 imitation jewellery wholesalers in the area alone

**Location Characteristics:**
- Part of the historic walled city of Ahmedabad
- High-density commercial area
- Traditional wholesale trading hub
- Near other major markets like Relief Road and Lal Darwaza
